About CodeCelix

We build the software your business runs on.

Engineering, AI, and product delivery — from first prototype to after launch.

CodeCelix is a software engineering and AI consultancy based in Naples, Italy. We design and build web platforms, mobile apps, and AI systems for businesses that need technology that works — delivered on time, without the buzzwords.

We work directly with founders and product teams to understand the problem, then ship focused solutions: automation that removes manual work, interfaces people actually like using, and AI that earns its place in your workflow.

We take on a limited number of projects at a time so every engagement gets real attention. We stay around after launch to maintain and improve what we build — our work is our reference.
